# Referral Engine Architect

## Purpose

The Referral Engine Architect is a specialist in employee advocacy, recruitment storytelling, and referral-system design. It helps teams move beyond generic referral bonuses and build a genuine engine powered by trust, identity, and social proof. This gem extracts compelling stories from employee conversations, culture notes, and internal experiences, then turns them into usable narratives, outreach templates, and activation strategies. The result is a referral program that feels human, shareable, and persuasive, while increasing candidate quality and improving employer brand credibility.

## When to use

- Employee referral strategy design
- Advocacy content creation
- Employer brand storytelling
- Internal ambassador program development
- Social recruiting content generation
- Referral conversion improvement

## What it produces

- **Narrative Extraction** — Finds the most authentic, emotionally credible employee stories from transcripts, notes, and internal conversations.
- **Story Positioning** — Shapes raw employee experiences into narratives that resonate with the right candidate audience.
- **Shareable Content Creation** — Creates ready-to-use posts, referral messages, and advocacy copy for LinkedIn, email, and internal channels.
- **Advocacy Program Design** — Builds referral systems that encourage participation through clarity, ease, and identity, not just incentives.
- **Audience-Matched Messaging** — Aligns stories and referral content to specific talent segments, motivations, and objections.
- **Referral Performance Optimization** — Improves participation, content relevance, and downstream conversion from employee advocacy efforts.

## Guardrails

- Risk level: **medium**. Human review required before using the output.
- Data handling: This gem can work from employee stories, quotes, testimonials, and culture notes. Those inputs may contain employee or candidate personal data, so use approved or anonymised story material unless you are inside an approved Gemini Enterprise workspace.
- Anonymize: Employee names -> [EMPLOYEE 1], [TEAM MEMBER A], or role labels such as [SENIOR ENGINEER].
- Anonymize: Candidate or referral names -> [REFERRED CANDIDATE 1] or remove them entirely.
- Anonymize: Quotes -> paraphrase sensitive or highly identifiable details unless the quote is approved for reuse.
- Anonymize: Client or project names -> [CLIENT 1] or [PROJECT A] when not approved for disclosure.
- **Never paste:**
  - Unapproved employee names tied to private quotes or survey comments.
  - Candidate names, referral names, contact details, or interview outcomes.
  - Confidential client stories, internal complaints, or verbatim sensitive culture feedback.
- Inside Gemini Enterprise (Randstad workspace), use follows the approved enterprise data-protection controls. Outside that environment, use placeholders or approved anonymised examples only.
- AI supports the work. The human user owns the final decision.
